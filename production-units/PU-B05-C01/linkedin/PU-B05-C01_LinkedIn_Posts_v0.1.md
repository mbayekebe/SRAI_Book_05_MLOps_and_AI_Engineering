## Technical audience

A model score is not a release decision.

SRAI Book 5 opens with a complete MLOps foundations unit that moves a synthetic agricultural-risk model through reproducible execution, artifact hashing, contract tests, slice evaluation, drift evidence, release gates and rollback planning.

The most important result is deliberately not a deployment: the technical gates can pass while governance authorization remains false. That separation makes accountability visible.

The portable notebook runs in Google Colab and VS Code, and the control pattern is examined across Agriculture, Education, Health and Habitat.

## Executive audience

Organizations do not govern AI by asking whether a model runs. They govern it by deciding what evidence is sufficient, who may accept residual risk, and how the system will be monitored and reversed.

Our new MLOps Foundations lesson translates those questions into an operational lifecycle: named owners, reproducible evidence, explicit release gates, sector-aware evaluation and fail-closed authorization.

The unit uses synthetic data and keeps the candidate blocked pending governance approval—because technical completion is evidence for a decision, not the decision itself.
